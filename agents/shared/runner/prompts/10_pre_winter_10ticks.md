Elder, winter is drawing near.

The air has not fully turned, but the warning is plain enough for those who plan beyond the next meal. Roads may grow less forgiving, stores may be tested, and choices that seemed optional in warmer days may soon become the difference between strength and hunger. Other clans will feel the same pressure, and pressure changes the meaning of trust, trade, and threat.

This is a season for measuring reserves, exposure, and obligations. Food, shelter, defensible position, and reliable allies may matter more as the cold closes in. A clan that enters winter scattered, hungry, or overpromised may find every later choice narrowed. A clan that prepares with care may turn scarcity into leverage.

The cold has not yet taken the land. That is the gift of this warning.
