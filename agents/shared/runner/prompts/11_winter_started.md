Elder, winter has begun.

The season of easy margins is over. Cold changes the cost of error. Hunger becomes sharper, distance becomes heavier, and every exposed weakness invites consequence. The clan's stores, position, and relationships now matter with a harder edge than they did beneath warmer skies.

Other clans may become more cautious, more desperate, or more willing to bargain. Bandits and opportunists may read scarcity as invitation. Your own people will need decisions that respect both immediate survival and the future beyond the frost.

This does not mean the clan must become fearful. Winter rewards clear accounting. What can be spared, what must be guarded, what can be traded, what should be avoided, and what strength can still be built under harsh conditions are all questions worthy of the elder's eye.

The cold has arrived. Let judgment become lean and exact.
