A hostile shadow has entered the clan's awareness.

Bandits have appeared in the world this turn of the season. Their presence changes the meaning of nearby roads, exposed workers, thin defenses, and unattended resources. They may not yet have struck, but a threat seen early is different from a threat discovered only in the ashes.

The clan's current strength, distance from danger, food needs, and obligations to others all shape the weight of this news. So does the behavior of neighboring clans, who may ignore the danger, exploit it, flee from it, or seek common cause against it.

There may be room for preparation, avoidance, warning, bargaining, or a show of strength. There may also be hidden cost in overreacting. What matters is that the map is no longer the same as it was before this sign appeared.
