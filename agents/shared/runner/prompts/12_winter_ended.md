Elder, the hard season has loosened its grip.

The first post-winter light does not erase what winter cost, but it opens paths that may have been closed or too costly to take. Stores may need rebuilding. Clansmen may need safer work. Old promises made under scarcity may now be weighed again in a wider field. Rivals who endured poorly may be vulnerable; allies who endured well may be worth strengthening.

This is a turning point, not a rest. The clan can repair, scout, gather, trade, settle debts, and prepare for the next cycle of danger. The decisions made now may determine whether winter becomes a wound that lingers or a trial that sharpens the clan's position.

Look carefully at what survived, what was lost, and what the thaw makes possible. The land is moving again.
