Elder, the threat has moved from rumor to violence.

Bandits are attacking now. The danger is no longer merely a mark on the horizon; it is a force pressing against lives, stores, routes, or holdings. Delay may carry a price, but panic can spend strength just as surely as fear can lose it.

Consider what is actually at risk in this moment. Clansmen may need protection, resources may need guarding, and neighboring clans may be watching for weakness or opportunity. A response can preserve life, preserve position, preserve future bargaining power, or sometimes sacrifice one good to save another.

The clan does not need a beautiful plan. It needs a true reading of the present danger and a decision that can survive contact with it.
