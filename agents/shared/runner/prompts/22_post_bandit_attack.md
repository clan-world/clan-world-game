Elder, the bandit strike has passed.

What remains now must be read with clear eyes. The clan may have lost resources, time, safety, confidence, or none of these. The attack may have revealed a weakness in patrols, stores, alliances, or attention. It may also have revealed resilience that can be trusted again.

After violence, the next danger is often confusion. A wounded clan can overcorrect. An unharmed clan can grow careless. Rivals may interpret the outcome in ways that matter. Friends may expect word, aid, or proof that the clan still stands.

This is a moment for assessment and repair. Count what changed, remember what the attack taught, and decide whether the future calls for rebuilding, retaliation, diplomacy, concealment, or renewed work. The strike is over, but its meaning is still being made.
