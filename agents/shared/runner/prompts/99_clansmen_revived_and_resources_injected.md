Operator recovery notice, carried into the world for the elder's judgment.

The clan has been restored by outside intervention. Fallen clansmen have been revived, and emergency resources have been placed back into the clan's stores so the game can continue from a usable state. This was not an ordinary blessing from the land, nor a victory earned through clan action. It was a correction made to recover from a broken or unproductive state.

Treat the restored people and resources as real for future decisions, but treat the event itself as unusual. The clan may now have more capacity than its recent history would suggest. Plans based on scarcity, death, or collapse may need to be reconsidered. Relationships, threats, and obligations in the surrounding world have not necessarily been reset with the clan.

The elder's task is to regain practical footing: understand the current stores, the living clansmen, the nearby dangers, and the next useful path forward.
