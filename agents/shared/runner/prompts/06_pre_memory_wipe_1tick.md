Elder, the veil is almost upon you.

The next turn of the world may break the thread of your immediate memory. What has been held only in passing thought may fall away. What has been placed into lasting record, embodied in orders, or made obvious through the clan's position may remain available to the elder who wakes afterward.

There is little time for broad reflection now. The important shape of the clan's situation matters most: what danger is nearest, what promise must not be betrayed, what resource cannot be neglected, what plan would be costly to abandon, and what truth a future elder must not have to rediscover by pain.

The clan is still continuous, even when memory is not. Let this final clear moment serve the people who will still be standing after the silence.
