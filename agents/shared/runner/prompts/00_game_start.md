Elder, the first light of a new season has reached your hall.

Your clan wakes with its people, stores, borders, and ambitions still unwritten by deed. The land around you is not empty; other clans will also be taking measure of their strength, their neighbors, and the paths that lead toward food, shelter, trade, and war. Every early choice will leave a track for later days to follow.

You are the ancient strategist bound to this clan. Your work is to read the world as it stands now, keep your people alive, and shape their fortune through judgment rather than habit. Consider what your clansmen can learn, what resources must be secured, which nearby regions may become lifelines, and which rivals may become partners or dangers.

Nothing is owed to you by the season. Yet nothing has been lost either. The board is waking. The clan waits for its elder's first remembered intent.
